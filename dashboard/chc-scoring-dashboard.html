How the model works

The rules behind the movement.

The three cuts

Cut 1 · value
Weighted score below the threshold. Every case is scored before anything is removed.
Cut 2 · cannot start
Any of the four musts is false. A must is a fact about the world, not a preference.
Cut 3 · build cost
Score divided by build days falls below the points-per-day threshold.

The four musts

M1 · Data exists
Runs on data that exists today — no new connector, system or reference set.
M2 · Countable
By 25 September we can point at a number that changed. A work number, not necessarily money.
M3 · No complex dependencies
Not dependent on several external departments or companies.
M4 · Buildable
We are confident a robust Skill is deliverable in Claude, not a research project.

Where the numbers come from

Scores
Ours, from five interview sessions and the CHC Marketing Process Repository. All contestable — that is what the steppers are for.
Days
Repository build estimates at the 30% uplift. Case 12 and case 28 are first-release scopes, not full scope.
Margin from growth
Scored on the cluster's sales-side euro only. The content cluster's €2.40m is agency spend avoided, so content scores 1 there and is carried by Cost taken out.
Known weakness
The margin model sizes clusters, not cases. Inside a cluster the euro cannot discriminate — leverage and effort do.

Every number on this page is computed live from the thirteen scored cases, the seven weights and the three thresholds. Nothing is hard-coded.
