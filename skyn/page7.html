Privacy policy

This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We collect as little as possible to run SKYN well.

1. What we collect

Account details you give us (name, email), the designs you upload and the SKYNs we generate, billing metadata from our payment processor, and basic usage and device data needed to keep the service reliable.

2. How we use it

To generate your SKYNs, run your account and billing, provide support, keep the service secure, and — only with your consent — to understand product usage through analytics.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with processors that help us run SKYN (hosting, payments, email) under contracts that require them to protect it, or where the law requires disclosure.

4. Retention

We keep your uploads and generated files while your account is active. Delete a design and we remove it from active systems promptly; backups age out on a rolling schedule.

5. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and object to certain processing. Email privacy@skyn.example and we’ll respond within the timeframes the law sets.

Cookie policy

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use a small set of them, and you stay in control of the optional ones.

1. Essential

Required to sign you in, keep your session secure and remember your basic preferences. The service cannot work without these, so they are always on.

2. Analytics

Help us understand which features are used so we can improve them. These load only if you allow them in .

3. Personalization

Remember choices like recently used palettes to tailor your experience. Optional, and off until you opt in.

4. Managing cookies

Change your choices any time in cookie settings, or clear cookies in your browser. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of SKYN from working.
