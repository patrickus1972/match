Privacy policy

This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We collect as little as possible to run SKYN well.

1. What we collect

Account details you give us (name, email), the designs you upload and the SKYNs we generate, billing metadata from our payment processor, and basic usage and device data needed to keep the service reliable.

2. How we use it

To generate your SKYNs, run your account and billing, provide support, keep the service secure, and — only with your consent — to understand product usage through analytics.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with processors that help us run SKYN (hosting, payments, email) under contracts that require them to protect it, or where the law requires disclosure.

4. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and object to certain processing. Email privacy@skyn.example and we’ll respond within the timeframes the law sets.
